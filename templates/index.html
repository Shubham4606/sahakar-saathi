General guidance from official documents, not legal advice. For your own case, check with your PACS secretary or the Registrar of Cooperative Societies.

What you can ask

Pick a topic to see an example question answered.

  • Cooperative laws and by-laws

    Your rights as a member, how the board and general body work, elections and audits.

  • Ministry of Cooperation schemes

    PACS computerisation, new multipurpose PACS, grain storage and national cooperatives.

  • PACS services

    Crop loans, seeds and fertiliser, storage, common service centres and more.

  • PMFBY crop insurance

    Who can enrol, what you pay, what is covered and how to report crop loss.

  • Loans and Kisan Credit Card

    How a crop loan works, interest, repayment and what to keep in mind before borrowing.

  • Complaints and grievances

    Who to approach first, and where to go if nothing happens.

How an answer is made

  1. You ask. Type, or press the microphone and speak in your language.
  2. We look up official documents. The assistant finds the matching pages in the Acts, by-laws and scheme guidelines it has been given.
  3. You get a plain answer. It is written in your language, with the names of the documents it used. If the documents do not have the answer, it says so and tells you who to ask.

At your PACS office

No smartphone? A voice kiosk at the society office lets you speak your question and hear the answer. It opens this same page, so the answers are always up to date.

Kiosk set-up: a Raspberry Pi or tablet with a microphone and speaker, running this page in full-screen mode.

SahakarSaathi is built for Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement SIH26088. It is an information tool and is not an official government service.
