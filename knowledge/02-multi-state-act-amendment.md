# Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act and the 2023 Amendment

The Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002 governs cooperative societies that work in more than one state. It was amended by the Multi-State Cooperative Societies (Amendment) Act, 2023 to improve governance, transparency and elections.

Main changes brought by the 2023 amendment, in simple words:
- A Cooperative Election Authority is set up to conduct and supervise elections of multi-state cooperative societies, so that elections are held on time and fairly.
- A Cooperative Ombudsman is appointed to look into complaints from members of multi-state cooperative societies.
- Reservation on the board: at least one seat for Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes and two seats for women.
- Cooperative Rehabilitation, Reconstruction and Development Fund is created to revive multi-state societies that are in loss.
- State cooperative societies can merge into an existing multi-state cooperative society under the conditions of the Act.
- Provisions on disqualification of board members and on transparency in the working of boards.

This Act applies only to multi-state cooperative societies. A village PACS registered under a state Act is governed by that state's Cooperative Societies Act.
