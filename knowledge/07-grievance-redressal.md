# Where to Complain: Cooperative Grievance Redressal

Follow the steps in order. Keep a copy of every letter and get a written receipt or a diary number.

Step 1: Complain in writing to the society. Give the application to the secretary and the chairperson of the board, and get a signed copy. Describe the problem, the dates and what you want.

Step 2: If there is no reply within a reasonable time (about 30 days), write to the Assistant Registrar or Deputy Registrar of Cooperative Societies of your district. The Registrar can order an inquiry or inspection and take action under the state Cooperative Societies Act.

Step 3: If the problem continues, write to the Joint Registrar or the state Registrar of Cooperative Societies. For disputes between a member and the society, the Act may require the matter to go to the Registrar for arbitration or to a cooperative tribunal or court.

For multi-state cooperative societies: complaints from members can be made to the Cooperative Ombudsman set up under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies (Amendment) Act, 2023, and to the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies.

Government portal: you can also file a complaint online on the Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System, CPGRAMS, at pgportal.gov.in, and it will be sent to the right department.

For a crop insurance (PMFBY) problem: call 14447, contact the insurance company or the district agriculture office, and then the District Level Grievance Redressal Committee.

For bank or loan problems with a cooperative bank: complain first to the bank's grievance officer. If not solved in 30 days, you can complain to the Reserve Bank of India through the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in.

For cyber fraud or online money fraud: call 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
