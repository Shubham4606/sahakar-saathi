# PACS (Primary Agricultural Credit Societies)

A PACS is a village-level cooperative society that gives farmers short-term credit and other services. It is the base of the three-tier short-term cooperative credit structure: PACS at the village level, District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCB) at the district level and the State Cooperative Bank at the state level. There are over one lakh PACS in India.

Typical services of a PACS:
- Short-term crop loans and Kisan Credit Card (KCC) loans.
- Supply of seeds, fertiliser, pesticides and farm tools.
- Storage and warehousing of produce.
- Marketing help for farm produce.
- Distribution of essential goods, in some places through the public distribution system.
- Enrolment help for crop insurance (PMFBY) for loanee farmers.

Under the Model By-laws for PACS (2023) prepared by the Ministry of Cooperation, a PACS can take up many more activities, such as dairy, fisheries, storage and warehousing, Common Service Centres, Jan Aushadhi Kendras, PM Kisan Samriddhi Kendras, LPG distribution, petrol and diesel outlets, and other services, so that it becomes a multipurpose society and earns more for its members.

Membership: usually a farmer or resident of the society's area of operation can become a member by applying to the society, paying the share amount and following the by-laws. Exact eligibility is written in the society's by-laws.
