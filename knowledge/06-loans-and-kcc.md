# Crop Loans and the Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

A Kisan Credit Card gives farmers easy access to short-term credit for crop cultivation and related needs from a cooperative bank, PACS, commercial bank or regional rural bank. It works like a revolving credit limit: you can withdraw when you need money for seeds, fertiliser or labour and repay after harvest.

Interest: crop loans up to a limit get an interest subvention (a government subsidy on interest) under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme. Farmers who repay on time also get an additional incentive on interest. With both benefits, the effective interest for timely repayers can be as low as about 4 percent a year on loans up to the eligible limit. The eligible limit was raised from 3 lakh rupees to 5 lakh rupees in the Union Budget 2025. Check the current limit and rates with your PACS or bank because they can change.

Collateral: the Reserve Bank of India has raised the limit of collateral-free agricultural loans to 2 lakh rupees per borrower. Check current rules with your bank.

Documents usually needed: application form, identity and address proof, land records or tenancy proof, passport size photo, and details of crops to be grown.

Before you borrow: borrow only what you need, know the interest rate and due date, keep receipts for every payment, and ask for a written repayment schedule. Repaying on the due date keeps your interest low and your credit record clean.

If a PACS or bank refuses a loan or delays it without reason, ask for the reason in writing and then follow the complaint steps in the grievance guide.
