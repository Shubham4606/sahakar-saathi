# PMFBY - Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

PMFBY is the government's crop insurance scheme. It gives financial support to farmers when the notified crop is lost or damaged because of natural events.

Premium paid by the farmer: 2 percent of the sum insured for Kharif food crops and oilseeds, 1.5 percent for Rabi food crops and oilseeds, and 5 percent for annual commercial and horticultural crops. The rest of the premium is paid by the Central and State governments.

Who can enrol: farmers growing notified crops in notified areas, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers. Enrolment is voluntary for all farmers, including those who take a crop loan. Farmers with a loan from a bank or PACS are usually enrolled through the loan account unless they opt out in writing before the cut-off date.

What is covered:
- Yield loss from non-preventable natural risks such as drought, dry spell, flood, inundation, pests and diseases, landslide, natural fire and lightning, storm, hailstorm and cyclone.
- Prevented sowing or planting when there is not enough rain or the conditions are bad.
- Mid-season adversity, where the expected yield falls sharply.
- Post-harvest losses for a limited period (generally up to 14 days) for crops drying in the field after harvest, caused by cyclone, cyclonic rain or unseasonal rain.
- Localised calamities like hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst and natural fire, assessed on the individual farm.

How to enrol: through your bank or PACS, a Common Service Centre, an insurance company agent, the PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in) or the Crop Insurance mobile app. Keep land records or tenancy documents, sowing declaration and bank account details ready. Enrolment must be done before the last date announced for each season in your state, which differs by state and crop, so check with the bank, PACS or agriculture office.

How to report crop loss (claim intimation): for localised calamities and post-harvest losses, inform within 72 hours of the event. You can do this through the Crop Insurance mobile app, by calling the toll-free helpline 14447, or by informing the bank, the insurance company or the agriculture department. Give your policy details, crop, survey number and a short description. Photos help.

After intimation, a survey is done and the claim amount is paid directly into the bank account if the loss is approved.

Complaints about PMFBY: first contact the insurance company or the district agriculture office. If the problem is not solved, use the District Level Grievance Redressal Committee, or call 14447.
