# Ministry of Cooperation: Schemes and Initiatives

The Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India, was formed in July 2021 with the aim of Sahkar se Samriddhi (prosperity through cooperation). Main initiatives, in short. Details and current status should be checked on the ministry website, cooperation.gov.in.

Computerisation of PACS: a project to bring PACS onto a common software system connected to NABARD and the cooperative banks, so that accounts, loans and services are digital and transparent. Around 63,000 functional PACS were approved for computerisation in 2022.

New multipurpose PACS, dairy and fishery cooperatives: a plan to set up cooperatives in panchayats and villages that do not yet have one, so that every panchayat has a PACS, a dairy or a fishery cooperative.

World's largest grain storage plan in the cooperative sector: a plan to build decentralised godowns and warehouses at PACS level so that farmers can store grain near their village and reduce losses and distress sale.

National multi-state cooperative societies set up in 2023: National Cooperative Export Limited (NCEL) for exports of cooperative products, National Cooperative Organics Limited (NCOL) for organic products, and Bharatiya Beej Sahakari Samiti Limited (BBSSL) for quality seeds.

Model by-laws for PACS (2023): allow PACS to take up many new activities so that they become multipurpose and financially stronger.

National Cooperative Policy: a policy document of the Ministry of Cooperation that sets the direction for growing the cooperative sector over the next years.

Other support: cooperatives can also access schemes through NCDC (National Cooperative Development Corporation) and NABARD.
